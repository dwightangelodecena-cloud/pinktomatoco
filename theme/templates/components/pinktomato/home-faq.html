What are your candles made of?

Coconut soy wax and fragrance oils that are paraben free, phthalate free, cruelty free and vegan, poured by hand in North Dakota.

Why does my wooden wick crackle?

That soft crackle is the wood burning, like a little fireplace. It's completely normal and part of the charm.

How long will my candle burn?

It depends on the size. Each product page lists its burn time.

Can I reuse the jar?

Yes. When the candle is finished, remove any leftover wax, wash the glass with warm soapy water and give it a second life.
