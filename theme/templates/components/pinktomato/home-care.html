Candle care

Get the best from every burn

  1. 01{{> components/pinktomato/icon name="flame" size="22"}}

    Let the first burn pool

    On the first light, burn until the melted wax reaches the edge of the jar, about 1 hour per inch of width. It stops tunnelling.

  2. 02{{> components/pinktomato/icon name="check" size="22"}}

    Trim the wick

    Trim wooden wicks to 1/8 inch and cotton wicks to 1/4 inch before each burn for a clean, even flame.

  3. 03{{> components/pinktomato/icon name="clock" size="22"}}

    Up to 4 hours at a time

    Burn for no more than 4 hours, then let the candle cool completely before lighting it again.

  4. 04{{> components/pinktomato/icon name="shield" size="22"}}

    Never leave it alone

    Keep candles away from drafts, children and pets, and never leave a burning candle unattended.
